# Audit checklist

Use this checklist when the user supplies a screenshot, existing listing, or
unsold post. The audit says what to change; it does not claim why the item
failed.

## Evidence labels

- **Observed:** directly supported by the screenshot.
- **Possible blocker:** plausible but not proven by the screenshot.
- **Unknown:** requires metrics, comparables, or user confirmation.

## Review dimensions

- **Search clarity:** Does the title identify the product and useful supported
  attributes in buyer language? Recommend the missing words or a clearer order.
- **First impression:** Is the item understandable at a glance from the cover
  photo and title? Recommend a clearer lead image or title when needed.
- **Buyer confidence:** Are condition, size, model, included items, price,
  delivery, payment, and location clear when relevant? Ask for unknown facts.
- **Readability:** Remove repetition, filler, crowded paragraphs, generic
  claims, hype, and machine-language patterns.
- **Conversion friction:** Identify questions a buyer must ask before deciding;
  add only answers supported by evidence.
- **Possible non-copy blockers:** Mention price, photo quality, category,
  location, low demand, age, views, saves, or messages only with the right
  label. Price is unchanged unless research was requested.

## Audit wording

Lead with 2–5 prioritized changes. Say what is unclear and what to change in
direct language, such as `Agregue color y tallas al título`, `Confirme el
modelo antes de incluirlo`, or `Move pickup and payment into the description`.
Use Observed, Possible blocker, or Unknown only when the label helps explain
the evidence.

Avoid administrative or customer-facing filler inside replacement copy, such as
`no hay evidencia`, `principal ventaja`, or `posible bloqueador`.

## Audit format

Then provide the complete replacement with the market-appropriate headings:

- Honduras or Spanish US: `Qué cambiar`, `Título mejorado`,
  `Descripción mejorada`, and `Etiquetas`.
- English US: `What to change`, `Improved title`, `Improved description`, and
  `Search terms`.

Unless the user requests final copy only, keep the audit bullets and the full
replacement together.
